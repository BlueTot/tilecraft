Welcome to your world!

This is Tilecraft, a simulation of minecraft but in 2 dimensions only.

The movement controls are:
W: Up
A: Left
S: Down
D: Right

The other controls are:
E: Inventory
H: Help
Q: Eat Food
T: Type Functions

When you open up your inventory (E), you will see the default inventory mode.
Access other inventory modes:
Z: Enchanting (Require Enchanting Table)
X: Smelting (Require Furnace)
C: Crafting (Require Crafting Table)
E: Exit Inventory (ANY MODE)
S:Hotbar 1
D: Hotbar 2
F: Hotbar3
G: Hotbar 4
H: Hotbar 5
J: Hotbar 6
K: Hotbar 7
L: Hotbar 8
Semi-Colon(;): Hotbar 9

Inventory Mode:
A: Armour
Z: Crafting 1st slot
X: Crafting 2nd slot
C: Crafting 3rd slot
V: Crafting 4th slot

Crafting Mode:
Z: Crafting 1st slot
X: Crafting 2nd slot
C: Crafting 3rd slot
V: Crafting 4th slot
B: Crafting 5th slot
N: Crafting 6th slot
M: Crafting 7th slot
COMMA(,): Crafting 8th slot
FULL STOP(.): Crafting 9th slot

You have a health bar and hunger bar. Walking will decrease hunger, where as eating will increase hunger.
Craft a crafting table to access other crafts. Use a furnace to smelt and an enchanting table to enchant.
Different tier tools will mine different amounts of wood and stone.
In game structures include:
	-Village (ORANGE)
	-Cave (LIGHT GREY)
	-Water Pool (LIGHT BLUE)
	-Lava Pool (RED)
	-Deep Cave (BROWN)
	-Ruined Portals (DARK PURPLE)
	-Bastion (YELLOW)
	-Fortress (RED-BROWN)
Use function input in console to type in commands. These are found by pressing 'f' in game.
To Speedrun the game, you have to find and play the pigstep disc. Enable the Speedrun datapack in the main menu and it will time your run!
FUNCTIONS:

Pressing 't' in-game will paste an 'input' into the console. Type your desired function in.
If you want to repeat your function a number of times, follow the syntax 'function, number of times'. This number must be in between 1 and 16.

ADVANCEMENTS:

	- Getting an Upgrade: Craft a stone pickaxe
	- Acquire Hardware: Get an Iron Ingot

	- Diamonds!: Get a diamond
	- Cover Me with Diamonds: Craft Diamond Armour
